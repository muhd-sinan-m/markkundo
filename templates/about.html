Academic Intelligence Platform

Intelligent Performance Analytics, Simplified.

markkundo. transforms raw internal assessment scores into meaningful academic trajectories. Working hand-in-hand with Padikkunnundo, it empowers students with automated marks sync, machine-learning diagnostics, cohort benchmarking, and optimized study planning.

Seamless Marks Sync

Instantly imports and normalizes all enrolled subject scores (ISA, CP, LB, LD, SEA1, SEA2) from Padikkunnundo without manual data entry.

Cohort Benchmarking

Evaluates individual normalized scores against the semester batch average, providing transparent visibility into subject strengths and growth opportunities.

ML Performance Profiles

Applies K-Means clustering algorithms to detect learning patterns, identify students requiring extra support, and celebrate top-tier mastery.

Priority Study Schedules

Calculates dynamic, credit-weighted weekly study hour targets prioritized by individual score urgency to balance workload effectively.
